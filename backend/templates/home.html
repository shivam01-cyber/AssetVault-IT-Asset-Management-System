FAQs

Quick answers before you sign in.

Who can access the system?

Guests can view this public page. Administrators and activated employees use their separate authenticated portals.

Can employees report asset problems?

Yes. Employees can report issues against assets currently assigned to them and follow the ticket lifecycle.

Are uploaded files public?

No. Ticket and profile files are stored outside the static directory and served only through authenticated routes.

What happens after an issue is reported?

The ticket can move through the helpdesk lifecycle while employees receive notifications about important status and reply events.

Can I contact support without an account?

Yes. Use the support contact details in the footer or the Ask AssetVault assistant for common questions.
